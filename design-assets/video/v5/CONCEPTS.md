# Alter hero film v5: three directions (~40s)
Tagline: Your bots work. You stay in control. CTA: Claim your founding spot.
Narrator reads "Altar" so it's pronounced AWL-ter. Every concept works muted: each VO line is also the caption.

---
## A. "Night shift" (quiet, emotional)
**Premise:** You sleep while your bots keep working. One small question is waiting for you in the morning.

**VO**
1. It's late. You're done for the day. [pause 1.0]
2. They aren't. [pause 1.5]
3. One researches. One writes. One passes the work along. [pause 1.0]
4. Nobody spends a cent without asking. [pause 1.5]
5. Morning. One question, waiting. [pause 0.8]
6. You tap yes. Or you don't. [pause 1.5]
7. Your bots work. You stay in control. [pause 0.8]
8. Altar. Claim your founding spot.

**Beats**
1. (0–5s) Dark HQ floor, one desk lamp lit, the camera drifting slowly. Bots seated calmly.
2. (5–11s) Bots come online one by one as real jobs start (status changes to working).
3. (11–17s) A handoff: a work packet moves from one desk to another, matching a real handoff event.
4. (17–23s) A bot reaches a purchase and pauses in its "needs you" state, the only vermilion on screen.
5. (23–31s) Phone Inbox at dawn with one approval card. The thumb taps Approve.
6. (31–36s) The floor settles. The sandbox card shows that bot's budget, still inside its limit.
7. (36–40s) Tagline, wordmark, CTA.

**Why it's different:** The emotion comes from rest and trust, with no crisis and no hook. It runs slow, with lots of silence.

---
## B. "Five words" (punchy, minimal, text-first)
**Premise:** Almost no narration. Big type and hard cuts to real UI, with one word per idea.

**VO** (sparse, or none; the type carries it)
1. Connect. [cut]
2. One paste. [pause 0.8]
3. Delegate. [cut]
4. They hand off. [pause 0.8]
5. Ask. [cut]
6. Anything that costs money asks you first. [pause 1.0]
7. Limit. [cut]
8. Every bot has a budget. When it's spent, it stops. [pause 1.5]
9. Your bots work. You stay in control. [pause 0.8]
10. Claim your founding spot.

**Beats**
1. (0–6s) "Connect." on black, then the connector URL pasted into an agent's settings and the bot appearing on the HQ floor.
2. (6–13s) "Delegate." The isometric floor in a wide shot, with one real handoff between two bots.
3. (13–21s) "Ask." A bot in its "needs you" state, then a hard cut to the Inbox approval card.
4. (21–29s) "Limit." The sandbox card with per-bot budgets. One bot reaches its limit and stops, its status shows stopped, and it sits idle.
5. (29–35s) All four words together on screen, then the calm floor.
6. (35–40s) Tagline and CTA.

**Why it's different:** It's structural and rhythmic, almost a feature manifesto. It is the strongest version muted and the shortest to say.

---
## C. "What didn't happen" (inverted)
**Premise:** The film is told backwards from a quiet ending. The story is everything Alter stopped. Nothing goes wrong, and that's the point.

**VO**
1. Here's what didn't happen today. [pause 1.2]
2. No surprise charges. [pause 0.8]
3. No bot wandering off with your logins. [pause 0.8]
4. No budget quietly gone. [pause 1.5]
5. Here's what did. [pause 1.0]
6. Your bots did the work. They handed it off. [pause 0.8]
7. And when it mattered, they asked. [pause 1.5]
8. Your bots work. You stay in control. [pause 0.8]
9. Altar. Claim your founding spot.

**Beats**
1. (0–4s) The end of a day: HQ floor calm, every bot idle, lights low. The camera holds.
2. (4–12s) Each "didn't" line plays over the real UI moment that prevented it, rewound: a denied approval card in the Inbox, an access request awaiting a tap, a bot stopped at its limit on the sandbox card.
3. (12–15s) A beat of black. The rewind ends.
4. (15–24s) Forward motion: jobs starting, bots switching to working, a handoff crossing the floor.
5. (24–31s) A bot asks; the phone Inbox shows the card, the owner approves, and the work continues.
6. (31–36s) Wide pull back to the full floor.
7. (36–40s) Tagline and CTA.

**Why it's different:** It's a negative-space structure that sells control through absence. The narrator's wry voice lives here.

---
Honesty notes: approvals appear when the user opens the Inbox (no push alert, so no notification banners). Handoffs are shown as work moving, not chat bubbles. Idle bots don't type. The sandbox card is labeled as a sandbox where it's shown.
