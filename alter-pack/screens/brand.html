Rules

  • Name is always lowercase "alter" in the lockup, "Alter" in sentences.
  • Type is Geist bold for the wordmark. No serif, no italics.
  • Red is the dot only. Never recolor the tray.
  • Dot means waiting. Use the idle mark when nothing waits.
  • Clear space is the height of the tray on every side.
  • Small sizes under 24px use the firm cut (favicon.svg).
  • On the card the mark is off-white #ECE9E2, top left, 40px.
  • Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or rotate it.
