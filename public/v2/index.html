07

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Alter is free for people.

Is my card safe?

There is no live card today. The card flow in the product is a sandbox, and no real money moves through it. A bot does not take your login. A spend that comes through Alter pauses until you approve it. Bills a provider charges on its own stay outside Alter.

What is a receipt?

A receipt records the job, the budget, the amount, who approved, when, and a SHA-256 hash of that snapshot. The hash shows the snapshot has not changed since it was written. It does not by itself prove the work, or a card charge.
