Questions

Free, the card, the receipt.

Is it free?

Yes. Alter is free for people.

Is my card safe?

There is no live card today. Card decisions run in a card sandbox, and no real money moves. A bot does not take your login. Bills a provider charges on its own stay outside Alter.

What is a receipt?

A receipt records the job, the budget, the amount, who approved, when, and a SHA-256 hash of that snapshot. The hash is verified from the snapshot. What the bot says about the work is reported, not verified.
