Today

What Alter does today.

Alter is the spending permission for AI agents: one budget and one receipt across every bot you run. Free for people.

HQ, the approval pause, receipts, and the founding-seat counter are in this repo. Pictures of the app are sample data. The receipt on this page is an example. The seat count is read from the server.

01

HQ

HQ is the workspace. It shows your bots, the jobs, and anything waiting on you. A new HQ starts empty. Opening it requires sign-in.

Sign in to HQ

02

Approval pause

When a rule is Ask, Alter pauses the action on the server. It stays paused until you approve or deny it. Nothing in that pause spends money. The card path is a sandbox.

Sandbox demo

The rows below are the server's own fixture at /api/authorization/demo. They are made up, they are not saved, and they use the same evaluator as sandbox card decisions.

Reading the sandbox demo.

Open the authorization page

03

Receipt

A public receipt shows the job, the budget, the approvals, and a SHA-256 hash of the snapshot. Run this job copies the template and the budget. It does not start a job, and it does not charge anything.

The document below is that same layout. It is an example assembled in the browser. It was not published from a live job.

Open the example receipt

04

Seat counter

The founding access list is a real counter. A seat is a place on that list. It is not access to the product, and it is not a payment.
