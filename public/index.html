Security

A connection is a narrow door, not a key to the building.

OAuth 2.1 with PKCE. Each bot has its own permissions: Auto, Ask or Never, per action. The kill switch stops every bot at once. Access tokens renew automatically in clients that support OAuth refresh. Authorization stays available until you revoke the bot.

A bot can never
×
Import your provider account.Your bot stays signed into its own provider. It never takes that login.
×
Read your other bots.A connection sees only the one bot you selected.
×
Move money.It cannot charge a card. The card sandbox is synthetic, and every real charge stays $0.
