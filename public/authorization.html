Bot trust

A signal only, never a score. It never changes what a bot is allowed to do. Shown after 5 sandbox decisions.

Issuer webhook adapter

A Stripe Issuing test-only signed webhook adapter is deployed at /api/issuing. It declines live-mode events and unknown or unapproved requests. The internal response deadline is 1 second. Stripe sandbox delivery and full round-trip timing are not verified until a test account and endpoint signing secret are configured. This is not live card interception.

What this demo does not prove

The scenarios use a made-up bot and job and are not saved. They call the same server evaluator used by sandbox card decisions. Merchant categories are reported by bots, not verified by a card issuer. Real card authorization, provider spend and model-token budgets are not connected.
