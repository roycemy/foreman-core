Alter HQ

Authorization engine / sandbox

Every decision has a reason.

Job budgets, merchant rules and request velocity are checked before simulated spend. Owner approval is always required. No card is issued and no real money moves.

Bot trust evidence

Score = 100 × (terminal decisions - policy blocks) / terminal decisions, with a minimum of 5 decisions. Owner denials are not policy violations. This is an early sandbox compliance signal, not a fraud model. It never expands permission.

What this demo does not prove

The scenarios use a made-up bot and job and are not saved. They call the same server evaluator used by sandbox card decisions. Merchant categories are reported by bots, not verified by a card issuer. Real card authorization, provider spend and model-token budgets are not connected.
